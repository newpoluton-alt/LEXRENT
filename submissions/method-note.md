# LEXRENT — challenge method note

Audit snapshot: **2026-10-04**. Default legal query date: **2026-10-01**.

LEXRENT serves renters, advocates/agencies and housing providers through **Module A** (atomic extraction), **Module B** (dated address evaluation) and **Module C** (change analysis). Next.js/React, Hono and a deterministic evaluator share one application; Neon provides identity, versioned storage and pgvector retrieval, while Claude supports extraction and cited research.

**Evidence and extraction.** The pack supplies 500 addresses, 87 source records and 568 captured law chunks. Supplemental primary sources retain separate IDs, URLs, retrieval times and content hashes. Claude processes overlapping chunks into cached, review-required drafts with model/usage metadata; logs retain progress and failures. Agent-assisted source analysis reviews dates, exemptions, scope and interactions before schema/exact-quote validation. Reviewed rule quotations are literal continuous captured spans. Draft cross-references establish evidence overlap, not semantic equivalence or unchanged model lineage. This is an agent-assisted audit, not independent professional legal review: `human_legal_review: false`. Unsupported drafts cannot replace published rules; uncaptured links remain gaps.

**Dated coverage.** The reviewed local bundle contains **95 atomic records: 40 CA, 30 NJ and 25 MA**, including city records and nonbinding proposals. All **500 addresses** receive state records and at least one current applicable record. Municipality evidence supports **447 addresses: 400 Census matches plus 47 manually reviewed NJOGIS/MOD-IV tax-parcel matches**; **53 remain unresolved**. All 47 complete CSV street strings match official `PROP_LOC` literally; municipal codes, names and parcel prefixes agree, and raw response hashes/attributes are verified. These records establish current municipal tax assignment, not surveyed boundaries, title or historical jurisdiction. Tax year is unknown. Typed true/false/unknown predicates preserve missing owner, occupancy, tenancy, certificate and exemption facts. Construction year cannot substitute for a certificate date. Conditional duties do not establish violations.

**Changes and deliverables.** All five local cases are evaluated: dated CA activation, isolated NJ city bans, future NJ FAIR interactions, pending MA bills and failed IP25-21. NJ FAIR activates **2027-07-01**; possible local conflicts receive review flags. T2 supports **90 affected addresses and 0 unresolved municipality cases**; its expected 90 remains a separate fixture value. Exports contain `rules.json`, all 500 IDs in `lookups.json`, all five cases in `changes.json` and a readiness report. These local results do not confirm deployment publication.

**Demonstration and limits.** Renter **A0001**, advocate **A0008** and provider **A0010** demonstrate evidence, conditional obligations and pending/future changes. Reproduce extraction, enrichment, review and exports using the [README](../README.md). Local exports need no paid model call or database; live overrides can differ. Every address retains at least one unknown requirement. Historical geography, uncaptured sources, missing facts and unmodeled exemptions remain explicit. `coverage_complete` stays false; evaluated cases and submission readiness do not certify complete legal coverage or compliance. Research only; not legal advice.
